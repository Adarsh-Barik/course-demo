🎯 COL1000 Word Guessing Game

How to play:

  • Guess the secret 5‑letter word in up to 5 attempts.
  • Your guess does not have to be a valid English word, but it must contain exactly 5 letters (A–Z).
  • Letters are compared by their Unicode value: A < B < C < … < Z.
  • After each guess you’ll see coloured tiles: Light Blue = guessed letter is smaller than the secret letter. Green = exact match. Strawberry Red = guessed letter is larger than the secret letter.
  • Use the on‑screen keyboard or your physical keyboard to type. Press Enter to submit.
  • Press ⌫ Backspace to delete the last letter.
